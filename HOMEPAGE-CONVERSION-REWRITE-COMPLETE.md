## 🎓 Conversion Psychology Principles Applied

1. **Loss Aversion** - "Don't risk family safety" more powerful than "Get safe windows"
2. **Social Proof** - 5000+ customers + 150+ reviews > "trusted brand"
3. **Urgency** - "Limited slots" + "24-hour response" drives immediate action
4. **Authority** - "24+ years" + "ISO certified" establishes expertise
5. **Contrast** - Poor contractors vs our solution increases perceived value
6. **Specificity** - "₹10,000 repair" more believable than "expensive"
7. **Emotional Triggers** - Family danger, money wasted move people faster
8. **Reciprocity** - "Free consultation, no obligation" creates obligation
9. **Commitment** - Multi-step form increases follow-through
10. **Consistency** - Values-aligned messaging increases trust
