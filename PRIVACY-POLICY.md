**Customer Information:**
- Active customers: Duration of service + 5 years (for tax compliance)
- Inactive customers: 3 years from last interaction
- Leads/inquiries: 2 years from first contact

**Website Usage Data:**
- Analytics data: 14 months
- Cookies: Duration set in cookie policy
- Log files: 90 days

**Communication Records:**
- Emails: 7 years (for compliance)
- Support tickets: 5 years
- Chat logs: 2 years

**Financial Records:**
- Invoices & receipts: 8 years (GST requirement)
- Payment records: 7 years (Income Tax requirement)
- Warranty records: Period of warranty + 1 year

### 6.2 Deletion Requests

You can request deletion of your information at any time. We will delete data upon request, except where:
- Required by law or regulation
- Needed for legal claims

- Business records retention is necessary

You have the following rights:
