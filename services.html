Tempered Glass Supply & Installation

Ganesh Glass Center supplies premium quality tempered glass (also called toughened glass) for all applications. Tempered glass is 4-5 times stronger than regular glass and designed to handle extreme temperatures and impacts.

Our tempered glass is manufactured following international safety standards and is certified for residential, commercial, and industrial use. We provide custom cutting and installation services.

Tempered Glass Applications:

  • Windows and door glass applications
  • Balcony railings and safety barriers
  • Shower enclosures and bath panels
  • Shelving and display applications
  • Furniture tops and table surfaces
  • Commercial storefronts

Safety Certified

All our tempered glass meets IS 2553 standards and international safety regulations. When tempered glass breaks, it shatters into small, harmless pieces reducing injury risk.
