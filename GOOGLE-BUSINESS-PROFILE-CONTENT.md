## ❓ Q&A Section (20 Questions & Answers)

### Understanding Glass Types & Options

**Q1: What types of glass do you use for windows?**

A: We use premium tempered glass, laminated glass, and tinted glass for windows. Tempered glass offers safety and durability (4-5x stronger than regular glass). Laminated glass provides soundproofing and security. Tinted glass reduces heat and glare - ideal for Ahmedabad's hot climate. Each type has specific advantages, and our team recommends the best option based on your location and needs.

---

**Q2: What's the difference between tempered and laminated glass?**

A: Tempered glass is heat-treated to be extremely strong and shatter-resistant, breaking into small safe pieces if damaged. Laminated glass has a plastic layer between glass sheets, providing soundproofing, security, and holding together if broken. Tempered is better for safety areas (balconies, showers). Laminated is better for security and noise reduction. We recommend based on your specific requirements.

---

**Q3: Is double-glazing available? Does it reduce electricity bills?**

A: Yes, we provide double-glazing (two glass panes with air gap) which significantly reduces heat transfer and noise. In Ahmedabad's hot climate, double-glazing reduces air-conditioning load by 20-30%, lowering electricity bills noticeably. It also maintains comfortable temperatures year-round. While installation cost is higher, energy savings justify the investment within 2-3 years.

---

**Q4: Can I get tinted glass? What colors are available?**

A: Yes! Tinted glass is available in multiple colors: Bronze (warm, popular), Grey (neutral), Blue (cool tone), and Green (light green). Tinting reduces heat by 40-50% and UV radiation by 99%, ideal for Ahmedabad weather. It also provides privacy while maintaining visibility. Tinted glass works well for windows, doors, and office partitions.

Your family's safety is our priority.

### Maintenance & Long-term Care

**Q16: How do I maintain my glass windows and doors?**

A: Regular maintenance keeps glass products looking new. Clean glass monthly with glass cleaner and soft cloth. Check seals quarterly for any cracks. Lubricate hinges and tracks annually. Address any issues immediately to prevent further damage. We provide maintenance guides with every installation. Annual inspection available - just call us.

---

**Q17: What if my glass gets scratched or cloudy?**

A: Minor scratches can often be polished out using professional polishing techniques. Cloudy glass might indicate seal failure (condensation between panes) - we can replace seals or full glass unit. Contact us for assessment. Most issues can be fixed cost-effectively, extending product life. Regular maintenance prevents most problems.

---

**Q18: Can you clean and maintain my glass regularly?**

A: Yes! We offer glass cleaning and maintenance services. Professional cleaning keeps glass crystal clear and extends product life. We can arrange monthly, quarterly, or annual maintenance contracts. Ideal for office buildings, shops, and commercial establishments. Competitive rates for regular maintenance packages.

Fully framed offers maximum durability and traditional look. Choice depends on your style preference, budget, and purpose. Our team recommends based on your space and needs.
