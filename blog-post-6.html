Aluminium doors are a popular choice for modern Ahmedabad homes due to their durability, aesthetic appeal, and low maintenance. Understanding pricing will help you budget your project accurately and make informed decisions when selecting doors for your home.

Factors Affecting Aluminium Door Pricing

Several key factors influence aluminium door costs:

  • Door type (sliding, folding, hinged, pocket)
  • Size and dimensions
  • Glass type (tempered, frosted, plain)
  • Aluminium profile grade
  • Hardware quality (locks, handles)
  • Finishing (anodized, powder coated)
  • Installation complexity

Installation Costs

Installation typically costs ₹50-100 per sq ft depending on:

  • Complexity of the project
  • Access to installation location
  • Need for frame repair or replacement
  • Additional finishing work

Money Saving Tips

Save money without compromising quality:

  • Compare multiple quotes from reputable providers
  • Order multiple doors for bulk discounts
  • Choose standard sizes instead of custom
  • Select basic glass options
  • Schedule installation during off-season

Remember that quality matters - the cheapest option may not be the best value. Always choose established providers like Ganesh Glass Center with proven track records and proper warranties.
