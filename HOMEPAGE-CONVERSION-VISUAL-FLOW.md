## 📱 Mobile vs Desktop Experience

### Mobile (70% of traffic)
- Sticky bottom CTA bar always visible
- Touch-friendly buttons
- Form with minimal fields
- Shorter copy blocks
- Emphasis on phone/WhatsApp CTAs

### Desktop (30% of traffic)
- Floating buttons on right side
- More detailed copy sections
- Full contact form
- Emphasis on comparison tables
- Multiple micro-CTAs per section

   Purpose: Danger, Emergency, Urgency

🟢 GREEN (#25d366)
   Purpose: Action, Safety, WhatsApp, Affirmative
   Used in: WhatsApp CTAs, Testimonial #2 accent, Conversion signals
   Effect: Trust + action signal + Indian cultural norm

🔵 BLUE (#1e40af)
   Purpose: Trust, Stability, Professional

   Effect: Corporate credibility, confidence

🟡 YELLOW (#fbbf24)
   Purpose: Warning, Attention, Scarcity
   Used in: "Limited Slots" badges, Hero alert box
   Effect: Scarcity trigger, draws attention
