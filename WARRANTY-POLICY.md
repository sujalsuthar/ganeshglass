## 2. Product Warranty

### 2.1 Warranty Duration

**2-Year Comprehensive Product Warranty**
- **Valid From:** Date of delivery/receipt
- **Expires:** 24 months from delivery
- **Coverage:** Manufacturing defects and material quality issues
- **Applies To:** All glass and aluminium products supplied

### 2.2 What's Covered

Product warranty covers:

**Manufacturing Defects:**
- Improper glass tempering
- Inferior glass quality
- Glass with air bubbles or impurities
- Uneven coloring or tinting
- Structural flaws in glass
- Defective sealing at purchase

**Material Quality Issues:**
- Low-grade materials supplied
- Non-conforming to specifications
- Substandard materials delivered
- Quality below promised standards
- Materials failing prematurely

**Damage in Transit:**
- Breakage due to courier/shipper (not customer handling)
- Damage caused by transportation
- Defects arising during delivery
- Proper packaging failure

**Defective Components:**
- Hardware defects (hinges, handles, locks)
- Frame defects (cracks, breaks)
- Seal defects (poor quality sealing material)
- Coating defects (paint, powder coat issues)

### 2.3 What's NOT Covered

Product warranty does **NOT** cover:

**Normal Wear & Tear:**
- Natural aging and patina
- Surface scratches from normal use
- Fading from sun exposure
- Slight discoloration over time
- Hardware wear from frequent use

**Improper Use:**
- Using products beyond intended purpose
- Misuse or abuse
- Impact damage from customer
- Dropping or mishandling
- Using with wrong tools or methods
- Using in unsuitable environments

**Environmental Damage:**
- UV damage from sun (not defect)
- Salt spray corrosion (coastal areas)
- Acid rain effects
- Pollution or chemical damage
- Weathering and natural elements
- Temperature-related wear

**Customer Modifications:**
- Unauthorized alterations
- Third-party modifications
- Cutting or shaping by others
- Drilling holes not authorized
- Painting or coating changes
- Component replacements by others

**Accidents & Acts of God:**
- Breakage from accidents
- Impact from objects
- Extreme weather damage
- Vandalism or theft
- Natural disasters
- Intentional damage

**Installation Issues:**
- Poor installation by others
- Installation not per specifications
- Installation by unauthorized persons
- Third-party installation errors
- Site condition damage
- Damage from other trades

**Maintenance Issues:**
- Lack of proper maintenance
- Failure to follow care instructions
- Use of improper cleaning materials
- Damage from insufficient cleaning
- Issues from ignored maintenance needs
- Damage from neglect

- Document any damage progression
- Keep all receipts and documentation

**Step 2: Contact Company**
- Email: warranty@ganeshglasscenter.com
- Phone: +91 98765 43210
- WhatsApp: +91 98765 43210
- Provide all documentation
- Describe issue in detail

**Step 3: Initial Assessment**
- Company responds within 24 hours
- Preliminary defect assessment
- Determines if in-warranty
- Schedules inspection if needed
- Provides timeline estimate

**Step 4: Inspection**
- Technician visits site (if needed)
