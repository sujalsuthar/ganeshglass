❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions from builders and housing societies

What discounts do you offer for bulk orders? +
We offer tiered discounts: 10% on 500+ sq ft, 15% on 1000+ sq ft, and 20% on 2000+ sq ft. For mega projects (10,000+ sq ft), we provide custom quotes with additional benefits like dedicated project management and extended payment terms.
What is your typical delivery timeline for bulk orders? +
Standard orders: 7-10 working days. For express orders, we can deliver within 3-5 working days. Delivery times depend on glass type, quantity, and installation complexity. We coordinate closely with your project timeline.
Do you handle complete installation or just material supply? +
We offer both options: material supply only or complete installation with labor. For bulk projects, we typically handle full installation to ensure quality and consistency across the entire project.
Can you provide customized solutions for complex projects? +
Yes! Our team specializes in custom solutions. We work closely with architects and engineers to design glass solutions that meet specific project requirements, building codes, and aesthetic preferences.
What payment terms do you offer to B2B clients? +
We offer flexible payment options: 50% advance, 50% on completion for standard projects. For long-term contracts, we provide extended payment terms (15-30 days) with proper documentation and credit verification.
Do you provide maintenance services after installation? +
Absolutely! We offer comprehensive maintenance contracts with monthly, bi-weekly, or weekly schedules. Our maintenance plans include preventive care, emergency support, and repairs with significant discounts.
What is your warranty coverage for bulk projects? +
We provide 2-year comprehensive product warranty and 5-year installation warranty. Warranty covers manufacturing defects, sealing issues, and installation quality. All claims are handled promptly with minimal disruption to your operations.
How do you ensure quality control for large-scale projects? +
Every project undergoes rigorous quality checks at multiple stages: material inspection, installation supervision, and final quality verification. Our ISO 9001 certification ensures consistent quality across all projects.
