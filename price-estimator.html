How Our Price Estimator Works

Our advanced price calculator considers multiple factors to provide you with the most accurate estimate possible:

  • Service Type: Different glass services have different pricing structures based on complexity and materials used.

Glass Types & Their Benefits

Normal Glass

Budget-friendly option, suitable for interior partitions and non-critical areas. Standard thickness 4mm-6mm.

Toughened Glass

4-5x stronger than normal glass, safer for families, meets Indian safety standards. Ideal for windows and balconies.

Tinted Glass

Reduces heat by 40-50%, provides privacy, 99% UV protection. Perfect for Ahmedabad's hot climate.

Important: Why Site Measurement Matters

While our price estimator provides accurate initial estimates, the final price will be determined after on-site measurement by our expert team. This is important because:

  • Precise Measurements: Actual site conditions may require adjustments to the estimated dimensions.
  • Design Complexity: Special shapes, curves, or custom designs may affect final pricing.
  • Accessibility: Building access, floor level, and installation difficulty impact labor costs.
  • Current Rates: Market rates may change; our team provides current market pricing during site visit.
  • Customization: Special requests like frosting, tinting patterns, or custom colors may involve additional charges.
