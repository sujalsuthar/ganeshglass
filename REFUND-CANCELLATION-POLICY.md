## 2. Cancellation Policy

### 2.1 Cancellation Timeline & Refund Structure

Refund eligibility depends on the stage of service:

#### Stage 1: Pre-Quotation Cancellation
**Timeline:** Before site measurement appointment

**Refund:** 100% of any advance payment
**Conditions:**
- No penalties or charges
- Refund processed within 7 days
- Cancellation via email or phone
- No reason required

---

#### Stage 2: Post-Quotation, Pre-Order Cancellation
**Timeline:** After site measurement, before formal order/booking

**Refund:** 90% of any payment received
**Deduction:** 10% administrative processing charge
**Conditions:**
- Site measurement conducted
- Quotation provided
- Cancellation before booking confirmation
- Refund processed within 14 days
- Written cancellation request required

---

#### Stage 3: Post-Order, Pre-Material Procurement
**Timeline:** Order placed, materials not yet purchased

**Refund:** 80% of advance payment
**Deduction:** 20% order cancellation charge
**Conditions:**
- Order confirmed in writing
- Advance payment received
- Material procurement has NOT started
- Cancellation request within 48 hours of booking
- Refund processed within 14 days
- Original payment method used

---

#### Stage 4: Material Procurement Phase
**Timeline:** Materials ordered/partially received

**Refund:** 50% of advance payment
**Deduction:** 50% for material costs and procurement charges
**Conditions:**
- Material orders already placed with suppliers
- Partial materials may have been received
- Non-returnable material costs non-refundable
- Refund processed within 21 days
- Customer liable for any material waste

---

#### Stage 5: Installation In Progress
**Timeline:** Installation has begun

**Refund:** NO REFUND
**Payment Due:** Full balance due for completed work
**Conditions:**
- Work already commenced
- Materials installed
- Labor costs fully incurred
- Customer must pay for completed work
- May be subject to additional costs for incomplete work

---

#### Stage 6: After Installation Completion
**Timeline:** Installation finished

**Returns/Refunds:** Not applicable unless defective
**Applicable Policy:** Warranty Policy instead of cancellation policy
**Conditions:**
- See Warranty Policy for product/installation defects
- Customer satisfaction period: 48 hours
- Defects must be reported within 48 hours

---

### 2.3 Company-Initiated Cancellation

The Company may cancel services under these circumstances:

**Due to Force Majeure:**
- Natural disasters (earthquake, fire, flood)
- Government restrictions or lockdowns
- Extreme weather conditions
- Pandemic or health emergency
- Other circumstances beyond control

**In Such Cases:**
- Full refund of all payments within 30 days
- No cancellation charges
- Company not liable for consequential losses
- Compensation not offered (act of God)

**Due to Customer Non-Cooperation:**
- If customer makes site access impossible
- Repeated reschedule requests
- Non-payment of agreed amounts
- Interference with work

**In Such Cases:**
- Company retains cancellation charges
- May claim additional damages
- Refund of balance if any after deductions
- Subject to dispute resolution

## 6. Returns

### 6.1 Product Returns (Defective Items)

**Eligibility:**
- Defective/damaged upon delivery
- Manufacturing defects discovered within warranty
- Non-conforming to specifications
- Covered under Warranty Policy

**Return Process:**
1. Report issue immediately (within 48 hours of delivery)
2. Provide photos/evidence of defect
3. Company arranges inspection
4. Replacement or refund decided
5. Defective item collected
6. Replacement shipped or refund processed

**Non-Returnable Items:**
- Custom-cut glass (used/installed)
- Special orders without defects
- Items damaged by customer
- Items past warranty period

### 6.2 Return Shipping

**Responsibility:**
- Company covers return shipping for defective items
- Customer pays for returns of non-defective items
- Packaging must be adequate to prevent damage
- Uninsured returns at customer's risk

## 8. Partial Refunds

Partial refunds apply when:

**Mixed Circumstances:**
- Some work completed, some cancelled
- Refund = Total payment - Completed work value - Applicable charges

**Calculation Example:**
- Total contract: ₹50,000
- 50% advance paid: ₹25,000
- Cancellation at Stage 3 (20% charge): ₹5,000
- Refund: ₹25,000 - ₹5,000 = ₹20,000

**Company Calculates & Communicates:**
- Detailed breakdown provided
- Itemized statement shared
- Refund amount clearly stated
- Payment processed accordingly

## 9. Special Situations

### 9.1 Project Postponement

**Option 1: Cancellation**
- Standard cancellation policy applies
- Refund per applicable stage

**Option 2: Postponement**
- Order held for 90 days (standard)
- Material reserved (subject to availability)
- No additional charges if within 90 days
- Beyond 90 days: Storage and handling charges apply

**Communication Required:**
- Formal written postponement request
- New timeline specified
- Agreement to postponement terms

### 9.2 Partial Project Cancellation

Some services cancelled, others proceed:

**Example:**
- Contract includes: Windows + Doors + Partitions
- Customer wants to cancel only Partitions

**Process:**
- Proportional refund calculated
- Only cancelled portion refunded
- Other services proceed as planned
- Adjusted final quote issued

### 9.3 Payment Dispute

If customer disputes charges:

**Company Investigation:**
- Review all documentation
- Verify quotes and agreements
- Check invoice calculations
- Confirm service completion

**Resolution:**
- If error by company: Refund overpayment
- If customer misunderstanding: Detailed explanation
- Disputes resolved within 30 days
- Escalation available if unresolved
